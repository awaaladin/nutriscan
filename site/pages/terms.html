1. What NutriScan is

NutriScan scores packaged food against WHO guidance on sugar, sodium, saturated fat and fibre, flags diabetic risk, adds personal notes based on information you give, and shows evidence about a product’s authenticity and data quality. It does not give nutrition scores to medicines.

2. Not medical advice

Everything in NutriScan, including scores, risk labels, personal notes and chat answers, is educational. It is not a diagnosis, treatment or prescription, and does not replace a doctor, dietitian or pharmacist. Always check the physical label. For medicines, doses, allergies, pregnancy or a diagnosed condition, ask a qualified professional. In an emergency call 112.

3. AI and estimated data

When a product is new we may use AI (currently Google Gemini) to read a photo and estimate its details, and the assistant uses AI to answer questions. AI can misread or misunderstand. Information from this route is marked “Estimated” until confirmed. Health scores come from fixed rules, not from AI. Verify important details on the pack.

4. Counterfeit information

NutriScan shows evidence about whether a product may be genuine, such as a captured or confirmed NAFDAC number and reports from other users. It cannot guarantee that any pack is genuine or fake, because counterfeiters copy barcodes and registration numbers. NutriScan is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NAFDAC. A product shown as “reported fake” reflects user reports and our review, not a legal finding.
