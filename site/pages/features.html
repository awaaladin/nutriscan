Features

Everything NutriScan does

A complete list, grouped by what you are trying to do. Items marked Planned are not in the app yet.

Scan & score

Understand any packaged food

Barcode scanning

Scan with your camera, or type the number if the code is worn or the light is poor.

WHO-based health score

A 1 to 10 score and a Good, Moderate or Avoid label from sugar, sodium, saturated fat and fibre per 100 g, with the reasons shown.

Diabetic risk

A separate read for people managing diabetes. It looks at sugar and net carbohydrates, and understands “sugar-free” claims and sweeteners.

Drinks judged as drinks

Beverages use stricter sugar limits than solid food, so a sweet soft drink cannot hide behind a small serving.

“Incomplete” instead of guessing

If the nutrition table is missing we do not invent a score. We ask for a photo of the table, or let you type the values.

Medicines handled differently

Nutrition scores do not apply to drugs, so we do not give one. You still get the authenticity information.

Personal

Advice that fits you

“For you” verdicts

Optional health details (diabetes, blood pressure, weight goal) adjust the advice on every product.

Allergy warnings

Ingredients are checked against the allergies you list. We only warn. We never promise a product is safe for an allergy.

History you control

See what you scanned, filter by Good, Moderate or Avoid, and delete any scan, or all of them, to free space. The product information stays in NutriScan, so nothing we learned is lost.

Insights and progress

Patterns in what you scan and goals to build better habits.

Works with poor signal

Recent scans are kept on your phone, so you can look again when the network drops.

Search without scanning

Look a product up by name, brand, barcode or NAFDAC number.

Authenticity & community

Fight fakes, together

NAFDAC number capture

Numbers are read from the pack or reports and format-checked. A number only counts as confirmed after our team checks it on NAFDAC’s official register.

Evidence-based authenticity

Not a vague percentage. A clear level (reported fake, disputed, verified, suspicious or unverified) with plain reasons.

Real or fake reports

Report with up to four photos, where you bought it and the batch number. One report per person per product.

Fake product alerts

A ranked list of products recently reported as fake, with the places they were bought.

Hard to manipulate

New accounts count for less. Photos and batch numbers count for more. Abusive reports can be flagged and hidden.

Fair to manufacturers

Makers can dispute a report, and a person on our team reviews it.

Assistant & voice

Ask, listen, understand

Ask NutriScan

A full-page chat for food and medicine questions. It is personal to you and puts NutriScan’s own data first.

Photos in chat

Attach up to three photos of a pack, a nutrition table or a medicine box. They are read for that answer and not stored.

Read aloud

Hear results and answers spoken, using your phone’s built-in voice. Turn on auto-read in Settings if you prefer.

Safe answers

No diagnoses, no personal doses. Emergency wording shows a fixed “call 112” notice.

Your chats, your call

Delete one chat or all of them. Using your chats to help improve NutriScan is off by default and you can withdraw at any time.

Local languages Planned

Translation and speech in Nigerian languages is something we want to build. It is not in the app today.

Our data

A Nigerian product data set that keeps growing

Add products with photos

Scan something new, take two photos, and it is added for everyone after you check it.

Cross-checked by research

New products are compared with other databases. Values are filled in only where sources agree.

Data trust on every product

Verified, corroborated, sourced or unverified, with the evidence and links shown.

Report a correction

Spot a wrong value? Tell us. Corrections need an independent source or our team before anything changes.

Learning from reviewed answers

The assistant prefers answers our team has reviewed and approved over its own guesses.

Our own model Planned

When we have enough reviewed data, we will train a NutriScan model on it. Here is the plan.
