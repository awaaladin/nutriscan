NutriScan (“we”, “us”, “the app”) helps you check packaged food and medicine and manage your diet and health goals. This policy explains how we handle your personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the regulations of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). We collect only what we need, for stated purposes, keep it safe, and give you control over it.

1. Who we are

NutriScan is the data controller for the personal data described here. To reach us, or our data protection contact, use the details on the Contact page.

2. What we collect

  • Account: username, email and password. The password is stored hashed, never in plain text.
  • Health profile: what you choose to tell us, such as your goal (diabetes, blood pressure, weight loss), whether you have diabetes or high blood pressure, height, goal weight, and notes such as allergies, conditions or medicines.
  • Health readings (Pro plans): weight, blood pressure and blood sugar readings you log, with the time and any note.
  • Scan history: the products you scanned, the score they received and when. A copy of recent scans is also kept on your phone.
  • Chats and AI tools: your messages to the assistant and its answers, kept in your account. Meal plans, reports and doctor summaries are created when you ask and are not stored by us. Photos you attach in chat are read for that answer only; we keep just a count.
  • Product photos: read to identify a product and its label. We keep the product facts, not the photo.
  • Reports and corrections: what you submit about a product, with the photos, place of purchase and batch number you add. Reports are visible to other users (see section 5).
  • Payments: the plan or credits you bought, the amount, the date and Paystack's reference. Your card or bank details go to Paystack, never to us.
  • AI usage: for each AI answer, which feature was used, the credits it cost and how much AI processing it took. This holds no message content.
  • Your agreements: when you accepted our Terms and this policy, and when you gave consent for your health information.

3. Why we use it, and the legal basis

  • To provide the service you signed up for (contract): your account, scoring, history, the assistant, Pro features, credits and payments.
  • To personalise advice using your health information (your explicit consent; see section 4).
  • To keep the product data set accurate and the community safe (legitimate interest): weighing reports, reviewing corrections and stopping abuse.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent fraud and manage fair use of the AI (legitimate interest).
  • To keep payment records and meet tax and accounting rules (legal obligation).
  • To send you emails about your account (contract): a welcome, payment receipts and password resets. We do not send marketing without asking.

We do not use your health information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

4. Your health information

Information about your health is sensitive personal data under the NDPA. We process it only with your explicit consent, which we ask for separately when you create your account, and only to personalise your scores, readings, plans and answers. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting the information or your account in the app (see section 10). Withdrawing it means we can no longer personalise your results, and it does not affect what we did before.

Health targets shown in the app are general guideline targets, not medical advice; your doctor's targets come first.

5. Who we share it with

We use service providers to run NutriScan. Each receives only what it needs and must protect it:

  • Google (Gemini AI): to read product photos and answer questions it receives your message, your health profile and readings relevant to the answer, and product facts, but not your name, email or username. In Research mode, Google also searches the web for the answer. Google processes this under its Gemini API terms.
  • Paystack: processes payments and receives your email and the amount. It handles your card or bank details under its own privacy policy.
  • Neon and Vercel: host our database, servers and uploaded report photos.
  • Our email provider: delivers account emails to your address.
  • Open Food Facts: receives a barcode or product name only, never personal data.

We may disclose information when the law requires it or to protect people's safety. Reports are public to other users: a report on a product, including your username, comment, photos, place of purchase and batch number, can be seen by others viewing that product. Do not put personal details in a report.

6. Transfers outside Nigeria

Our providers (Google, Vercel, Neon, Paystack and our email provider) may store or process data outside Nigeria, including in the United States and Europe. We transfer data abroad only as the NDPA allows: to providers bound by contracts and security standards that protect it at a level comparable to the NDPA, and, for your health information, on the basis of your explicit consent.

7. Improving NutriScan (opt-in)

You can let NutriScan learn from your chats. This is off by default. If you turn it on, an answer you rated helpful may, after our team reviews and edits it, become a general answer the assistant uses for everyone, and may be used to evaluate or train our AI. Before any use, identifiers are removed and emails, phone numbers and long numbers are scrubbed, and questions about you personally are left out. Scrubbing is best-effort, so please do not put details you want private in chats. You can turn this off at any time; your choice when you wrote a message is what counts for that message.

8. The shared product data set

When you add a product or a correction, the product information (name, brand, nutrition, ingredients, NAFDAC number and where each fact came from) becomes part of the shared NutriScan data set. It describes the product, not you, and stays if you delete your history or account, without any link back to you.

9. How long we keep it

  • Account, health profile and readings: until you delete them or your account.
  • Scan history and chats: until you delete them or your account.
  • Payment records: six years, as tax and accounting law requires. When you delete your account they are kept without your name or contact details.
  • AI usage records: kept for cost accounting, without your identity once your account is deleted.

10. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. In the app you can:

  • See and change your profile, health notes and readings.
  • Delete readings one by one, your scan history (History → Clear all history) and your chats (one by one or all at once).
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete my account. This erases your profile, health information, readings, chats, scans and reports straight away.
  • Turn off use of your chats for improving NutriScan at any time.

For a copy of your data, or anything else, contact us (see Contact). We will respond within 30 days. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

11. Security and breaches

Passwords are hashed, all traffic uses HTTPS, health information and chats are visible only to your own account, and our staff tools do not show your chats or readings. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the NDPC within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay, as the NDPA requires.

12. Children

NutriScan is for people aged 18 and over. Under the NDPA a child's data needs a parent's or guardian's consent, which the app cannot collect, so we do not knowingly accept children's accounts. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes and contact

We will update this page as NutriScan changes and show the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your health information, we will ask for your consent again in the app. For questions or requests, see Contact. Read also our Terms and Safety and compliance page.
