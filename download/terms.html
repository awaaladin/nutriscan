1. What NutriScan is

NutriScan scores packaged food against WHO guidance on sugar, sodium, saturated fat and fibre, flags diabetic risk, personalises notes from the information you give, shows evidence about a product's authenticity, and offers an AI assistant and, on Pro plans, health trackers and AI-written plans and reports. It does not give nutrition scores to medicines.

2. Not medical advice

Everything in NutriScan, including scores, risk labels, health targets, readings summaries, meal plans, reports, doctor summaries and chat answers, is general information for education. It is not a diagnosis, treatment or prescription, and it does not replace a doctor, dietitian or pharmacist. Do not start, stop or change a medicine or treatment because of the app. Targets shown are general guideline targets; your doctor's targets come first. Always check the physical label. In an emergency call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

3. AI, research and estimated data

The assistant, Research mode, meal plans, reports and product identification use AI (currently Google Gemini). AI can misread, misunderstand or be wrong, including when it cites sources. Research mode searches the web and prefers trusted health sources, but we cannot guarantee that any source or summary is accurate, complete or current. Product details found by AI are marked “Estimated” until confirmed. Health scores come from fixed rules, not from AI. Check anything important with a professional and on the pack.

4. Counterfeit information

NutriScan shows evidence about whether a product may be genuine, such as a NAFDAC number and reports from other users. It cannot guarantee that any pack is genuine or fake, because counterfeiters copy barcodes and registration numbers. NutriScan is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NAFDAC. A product shown as “reported fake” reflects user reports and our review, not a legal finding.

7. Pro plans

NutriScan is free to use with a monthly allowance of AI credits. Paid plans add features and more credits. Each plan includes everything in the plans below it:

  • Pro, ₦3,000 a month or ₦30,000 a year: 250 AI credits a month, Research mode, your avoid-list and scan patterns, and the weight tracker.
  • Pro Plus, ₦5,000 a month or ₦50,000 a year: 500 AI credits a month, plus the blood pressure tracker, 7-day meal plans and weekly progress reports.
  • Pro Care, ₦7,000 a month or ₦70,000 a year: 750 AI credits a month, plus the blood sugar tracker, glucose-aware plans and reports, and the doctor-visit summary.

The price shown in the app when you pay is the price you pay. A plan starts when your payment is confirmed and lasts 30 days (monthly) or 365 days (yearly). Paying again for the same plan, or for a bigger one, adds the new period after your current one ends; a bigger plan's features apply straight away. You can move to a smaller plan once your current one ends. We may change prices or features for future purchases and will show the change in the app before you pay; a period you have already paid for is not affected.

8. AI credits and fair use

  • Each AI action uses credits: a chat answer 1, a chat answer about photos 2, a question about a product 1, a Research mode answer 10, a meal plan, weekly report or doctor summary 3. Adding a missing product is free. The app shows your balance.
  • Your plan's credits renew on the first day of each month (Lagos time). Unused monthly credits do not carry over. Free accounts get 25 credits a month.
  • You can buy 100 extra credits for ₦800. These do not expire, are used after your monthly credits, and stay with your account while it exists.
  • Credits are charged only when you receive an answer. A failed answer costs nothing.
  • To keep the AI fast for everyone, each account can use up to 40 credits in any hour. If you reach that, the app tells you how many minutes until you can continue.
  • Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged for money.
