What NutriScan is

NutriScan scores packaged food against WHO sugar, sodium, saturated fat, and fiber thresholds, and flags diabetic risk. For items it identifies as medicine or another non-food product, it does not provide a health score — nutrition scoring does not apply to drugs.

Educational use only — not medical advice

Scores, risk labels, and AI-generated explanations are for general education. They are not a medical diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or substitute for a doctor, dietitian, or pharmacist. Always check the physical product label and consult a qualified professional for health decisions, especially regarding medication dosage, allergies, or managing a diagnosed condition.

AI-identified and estimated data

When a barcode isn't in our database, NutriScan may use AI (Google Gemini) to identify the product from a photo and estimate its nutrition facts by reading the label. This is best-effort: photos can be blurry, angled, or show an outdated label, and the AI can misread or misidentify a product. Results from this path are marked "estimated" in the app — always verify against the physical package before relying on them.
