Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

NutriScan ("we", "our", "the app") helps you check packaged food and medicine barcodes against WHO nutrition guidance. This page explains what we collect, why, and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account info: username, email, and password (stored hashed, never in plain text).
  • Health profile: optional flags you set yourself (e.g. diabetic, hypertensive) and your primary health goal, used only to personalize risk messaging within the app.
  • Scan history: barcodes you scan, the resulting score, and when you scanned them, so your History and Progress screens work and so products don't need to be re-identified.
  • Product photos: when a barcode isn't recognized, the photo you take may be sent to Google's Gemini API to identify the product and read its nutrition label. We don't store this photo after processing; only the resulting product name, brand, and nutrition data are saved.

Third parties we share data with

  • Open Food Facts — we query their public database by barcode; no personal data is sent.
  • Google Gemini — receives a product photo (not linked to your identity) and the already-computed score/label when generating plain-language explanations or identifying a product.
  • Neon (Postgres hosting) and Vercel — host our database and backend.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your health profile or scan history with advertisers.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see below). Health profile flags can be changed or cleared anytime from Profile settings.

Not medical advice

NutriScan's scores and risk labels are educational, based on WHO nutrition thresholds. They are not a diagnosis, prescription, or substitute for advice from a doctor or pharmacist.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: reach out via the app's Settings screen.
