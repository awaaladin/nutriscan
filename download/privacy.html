Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Applies to the NutriScan beta.

Contents 1. What we collect2. How we use it3. Who we share it with4. Improving NutriScan (opt-in) 5. The shared product data set6. Your choices and rights7. Keeping it safe8. Children9. Changes and contact

NutriScan (“we”, “us”, “the app”) helps you check packaged food and medicine. This policy explains how we handle your personal data. We design around the principles of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: only what we need, for stated purposes, kept safe, and under your control.

1. What we collect

  • Account: username, email and password. The password is stored hashed, never in plain text.
  • Health profile (optional): things you choose to tell us, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, a weight goal, allergies, conditions or medicines you list. This is sensitive information. You decide whether to provide it.
  • Scan history: the barcodes you scanned, the score they received and when. A copy of recent scans is also kept on your phone.
  • Photos for identifying a product: read to identify the product and its nutrition label. We do not keep the photo. We keep the resulting product facts.
  • Chat: your messages and the assistant’s answers, kept in your account. Photos you attach in chat are read for that answer and not stored; we keep only a count.
  • Reports: real or fake reports you submit, with the photos, place of purchase and batch number you choose to add, and any corrections you suggest. Reports are visible to other users (see section 3).
  • Feedback: thumbs up or down on assistant answers.
  • Purchases: if you buy Pro, Google Play confirms the purchase to us. Payment details stay with Google.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, score products, personalise results, show your history and answer your questions.
  • To keep the data set accurate and the community safe: weigh reports, review corrections and moderate abuse.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not use your health profile for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

3. Who we share it with

We use service providers to run NutriScan. They receive only what they need:

  • Google (Gemini): receives a product photo or your chat message, and the score or product facts needed to answer. It is not sent with your name. Google processes it under its own terms.
  • Open Food Facts: receives a barcode or product name. No personal data.
  • Neon and Vercel: host our database, servers and uploaded report photos.
  • Google Play: handles Pro payments.

We may disclose information if the law requires it. Reports are public to other users. A report you submit on a product, including your username, your comment, the photos, the place you bought it and the batch number you add, can be seen by other people viewing that product. Do not put personal details in a report.

4. Improving NutriScan (opt-in)

You can allow NutriScan to use your chats to improve its answers. This is off by default. If you turn it on, a chat may, after review by our team, be used as an example. Before use, identifiers are removed and emails, phone numbers and long numbers are scrubbed. This scrubbing is best-effort, so please do not put sensitive details in chats. You can turn this off at any time and delete your chats. Your choice at the time you wrote a message is what counts for that message.

5. The shared product data set

When you add a product or a correction, the product information (name, brand, nutrition, ingredients, NAFDAC number and where each fact came from) becomes part of the shared NutriScan data set. It describes the product, not you. If you delete your history or your account, this product information stays, so that what the community has built is not lost. It is not linked back to you after your account is deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and change: view and edit your profile in the app.
  • Delete your history and chats: in the app, History → Clear all history removes your scan history, including the copy on your phone. Chats can be deleted one by one or all at once.
  • Delete your account: ask us (see Contact) and we will remove the personal data tied to your account.
  • Withdraw consent for training use at any time.
  • Complain: you may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

How long we keep things: your account data until you ask us to delete it; scan history and chats until you delete them.

7. Keeping it safe

Passwords are hashed and traffic to our servers uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise it. If something affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

8. Children

NutriScan is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page as NutriScan changes and show the date at the top. For questions or requests, see Contact. Read also our Terms and Safety and compliance page.
