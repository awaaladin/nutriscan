Our data & AI

A data set we can stand behind

NutriScan is building its own Nigerian product data set, and an assistant that learns from it. Here is how, and the rules that keep it honest.

The problem

Global databases barely know Nigeria

Open databases such as Open Food Facts are a great start, but coverage of Nigerian brands is thin. The products people actually buy are often missing, and the ones that exist are sometimes for a different country’s version of the pack.

So NutriScan grows its own data set, one scanned product at a time. The hard part is not collecting data. It is knowing which data to believe.

What the data set holds

  • Products: name, brand, barcode, type
  • Nutrition per 100 g and ingredients
  • NAFDAC numbers and whether they are confirmed
  • Evidence: where each fact came from
  • Community reports and reviewed corrections
  • Reviewed questions and answers for the assistant

It holds product facts, plus the photos people choose to attach to a fake-product report. Your health details and chats are kept apart from it.

How a record is built

From a scan to a trusted entry

Every product follows the same path. Nothing skips a step.

1. A person scansA new product is photographed and read by AI. It is saved as “Estimated”.
2. We cross-checkOther databases are searched by name, brand and country.
3. Sources are comparedValues must agree. Different country means reference only.
4. Trust is assignedVerified, corroborated, sourced or unverified, with the reasons.
5. It joins the data setNext time anyone scans it, the answer is instant.

Country-aware

A listing from another country is never used to fill in a Nigerian product. The barcode prefix and the pack tell us where it belongs.

Never overwrites

Research can fill a blank only when sources agree. It never replaces a value we already hold. A real conflict is sent for review instead.

AI with citations only

AI web research counts as evidence only when it cites live pages. An AI answering from memory is never treated as a source.

Data trust

Four levels, and you can see why

The level is calculated from the evidence we hold. It is never set by hand to make a product look better.

Verified

The NAFDAC number was confirmed on the official register.

Corroborated

Two independent sources agree on the product’s values.

Sourced

One source we can cite supports it.

Unverified

Not enough proof yet. We say so plainly.

Why votes are not enough

Popular is not the same as true

Anyone can create accounts, and a crowd can be wrong or paid. So in NutriScan a vote never changes a product’s data on its own.

  • A correction needs enough credible people. Brand-new accounts count for very little.
  • And it needs an independent source that backs the same value. A database, a cited page or a label photo.
  • Otherwise it waits for a person on our team to review it.

Ten accounts agreeing on a wrong sugar value change nothing. We test for exactly that.

A correction, step by step

1

You report a value

For example, “the sugar is 14 g, not 4 g.”

2

It is recorded as a vote

Weighted by how established the account is.

3

We look for proof

An independent source, or our reviewer.

4

Only then it changes

And the evidence is recorded on the product.

NutriScan AI

How the assistant learns from our data

We want an AI that knows Nigerian food and medicine, and that gets better as the data set grows. We are doing this in honest stages, and we will say which stage we are at.

Today

Our data first

The assistant is told to rely on NutriScan’s own scores and trust levels. It also reads answers our team has reviewed and approved, and prefers them to its own guesses. Cloud AI from Google does the language work.

Building

A reviewed training set

Helpful chats from people who opted in are anonymised and put in a queue. Our team reviews them. Only approved examples count. A readiness report tells us when there is enough.

Planned

A NutriScan model

When there are thousands of reviewed examples, we will train a model on them. We will not train on unreviewed data, or on anyone’s chats without their opt-in.

Your say. Sharing your chats to improve NutriScan is off by default. You can turn it on or off in the app at any time, and you can delete your chats whenever you like.

What AI does here, and does not. AI reads labels, explains results and answers questions. The health score always comes from fixed, published rules. See how we use AI.
