About

Helping people choose what they eat and take, with confidence

NutriScan is a Nigeria-first app for understanding packaged food and medicine, and for spotting the fakes.

What NutriScan is

A scanner, a guide and a growing record

You scan a barcode. NutriScan tells you what is in the product, what it means for your health, and how far its information can be trusted. If a product is new, you help add it, and everyone after you benefits.

Behind the app is a data set built for Nigerian products, and an assistant that answers questions in plain language. Each part is designed so that you can see why it says what it says.

Why it exists

Three problems, one app

Labels are hard

Nutrition tables are small and technical. Most people cannot tell, in a shop, whether a snack is a sensible choice for them.

Fakes look real

Look-alike packs of food and medicine reach shelves and markets. Ordinary shoppers have almost no way to check.

Local data is missing

Global databases know few Nigerian products. People need information about what they actually buy.

What we believe

Principles we hold ourselves to

Truth over polish

If we do not know, we say so. We would rather show “unverified” than a confident number we cannot support.

Show the evidence

Every important claim comes with its reasons, so you can judge it yourself.

Rules decide, AI assists

Scores come from fixed, published guidance. AI helps read and explain. It does not decide.

Your data is yours

Health details are optional, sharing for training is opt-in, and you can delete your history.

Where we are going

Our roadmap, honestly labelled

Now

Beta on Android

Scanning, scores, personal advice, the assistant, product photos, reports and the trusted data set are in the app today.

Next

Play Store release

A signed public release, working Pro payments through Google Play, and wider Nigerian product coverage.

Later

A NutriScan model and local languages

A model trained on our reviewed data, and speech and translation in Nigerian languages. These are goals, not promises of a date.
