Contact

We want to hear from you

Pick what you need. The fastest route is often inside the app.

Report a fake product

Open the product in the app and tap Report. Add photos, where you bought it and the batch number. If you think it is dangerous, tell NAFDAC through its official channels too.

Correct a product

On any product, tap Report a correction and enter the right value from the pack. We check it against independent sources before changing anything.

Delete my history or chats

You can do this yourself: History → Clear all history, and delete chats from the chat list. No request needed.

Delete my account and data

Email us from the address on your account and ask for deletion. We will confirm and remove the personal data tied to it. See the Privacy Policy.

Report a bug

Tell us your phone model, Android version and what happened. Screenshots help.

Partnerships, press, manufacturers

Makers can dispute a report about their product, and organisations can talk to us about data and safety work.

Get in touch

Write to the team

Email: our public support address is being set up for the public launch. Until then, beta testers can reach the team through whoever shared the app with them.

Please do not send passwords, full card details or medical records. For a medical emergency, call 112.

Useful to include

  • What you were doing and what you expected
  • The product name and barcode, if it is about a product
  • Your phone model and Android version, for bugs
  • The username on your account, for account requests
Quick answers

Before you write

The scanner does not read my barcode

Hold the phone steady, add light and fill the frame with the barcode. If it still fails, tap Type the barcode instead.

My product says “Incomplete”

The nutrition table is missing, and we do not score on missing data. Tap Photograph the table or type the values in from the pack.

A product score looks wrong

Check the values against the pack. If they differ, use Report a correction. The score is calculated from those values, so fixing them fixes the score.
