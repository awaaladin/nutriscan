What you should know before you rely on NutriScan

Plain answers about medical advice, AI, NAFDAC, your data and the services we use. Read this before you download.

Not medical advice

NutriScan gives educational information based on public WHO nutrition guidance. It is not a diagnosis, a treatment or a prescription, and it does not replace a doctor, dietitian or pharmacist.

  • Always read the physical label. Packs change, and a scan can be out of date.
  • For medicines, doses, interactions, pregnancy, allergies or a diagnosed condition, ask a qualified professional.
  • The app does not identify tablets by how they look, and does not give personal doses.

In an emergency (a suspected overdose, chest pain, trouble breathing, a severe allergic reaction), call 112 or go to the nearest hospital. Do not use an app.

How we use AI

We use AI in three places, and we want you to know which.

  • Reading photos. When a product is new, AI reads the pack and nutrition table. Results are marked “Estimated” until confirmed.
  • Explaining and chatting. The Ask NutriScan assistant answers in plain language. It is told to treat NutriScan’s own scores and trust levels as the source of truth.
  • Research. AI web research counts only when it cites live pages.

What AI does not do: it does not set the health score. The score comes from fixed rules based on WHO thresholds, so the same product always gets the same score.

AI can be wrong. It can misread a blurred photo or misunderstand a question. That is why we show evidence and trust levels, mark estimates, and ask you to check the pack.

We currently use Google’s Gemini models for the language and vision work. Product photos sent for reading are not linked to your name.

NAFDAC and authenticity

NutriScan is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or a service of NAFDAC.

  • A NAFDAC number is captured from the pack or from reports and format-checked. NAFDAC has no public API, so a number counts as confirmed only after a person on our team checks it on NAFDAC’s official online register.
  • Counterfeiters can copy real barcodes and real NAFDAC numbers. So a captured or even confirmed number is not proof that a particular pack is genuine.
  • We show evidence: the registry status, what others reported, and the reasons. We never say a product is “100% genuine”.
  • If you think a product is fake or dangerous, tell NAFDAC through its official channels as well as reporting it in the app.

Your data and your rights

We design NutriScan around the principles of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: collect only what we need, tell you why, keep it safe, and respect your choices.

  • Health details are optional and are used only to personalise results. Using your chats to help improve NutriScan is off by default.
  • You can see and change your profile at any time.
  • You can delete your scan history and your chats in the app. The product information we learned stays in the shared data set, because it describes the product, not you.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data tied to it. See Contact.
  • Passwords are stored hashed. Traffic to our servers uses HTTPS.
  • NutriScan is not meant for children under 13.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Reports and moderation

  • Report only what you honestly believe. Reports can be flagged by other users, and our team can hide fake or abusive ones so they do not count.
  • One report per person per product. New accounts count for less than established ones.
  • Manufacturers can dispute a report. A person on our team reviews disputes, and we correct the record when a report is wrong.
  • Do not upload photos of people, or anything that is not the product.
  • There is no public comment feed. Reports feed a safety list, not a debate.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data, or share your health profile or scan history with advertisers.
  • We do not claim a product is certified, approved or guaranteed genuine.
  • We do not give a score to a product when we lack the data to judge it.
  • We do not let votes or payments change a product’s data or score.
